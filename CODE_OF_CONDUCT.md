# Code of conduct

CSX welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, give constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on the work. Make room for questions and different perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. These standards apply in CSX issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness a breach of this code, contact [Floffah](https://github.com/Floffah) privately. If you need a private contact channel, open an issue asking for one and keep names and incident details out of the issue. You can also [report abusive content to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies#how-to-report-abuse) directly.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly. Depending on the severity and whether the behaviour is repeated, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
