# AI-assisted contributions

AI tools are welcome in CSX. The same review requirements apply to every contribution.

1. **Before opening a pull request**, personally review the entire diff, understand the changes, and run the relevant checks yourself. You are responsible for explaining the changes and addressing review feedback.
2. **Before merging**, a maintainer or authorised contributor other than the author must review and approve the changes.

Automated checks and AI reviews can support both reviews. Both reviews must be performed by people.
